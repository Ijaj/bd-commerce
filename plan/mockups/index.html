Launch / Full phase switch

The "Launch / Full" switch (bottom right, next to the brand buttons; also ?phase=launch|full, remembered in localStorage, default Launch) shows what the shop looks like at launch versus later.

Launch (COD only)
  • Only cash on delivery in checkout; no bKash / Nagad / gateway methods, no method discounts or cashback banner
  • No advance payment anywhere: the delivery fee is collected on delivery, no "Pay now" line
  • Pre-order is hidden: no pre-order badges, product.html?p=preorder shows "Coming soon / Notify me"
  • payment.html shows "Online payments not enabled at launch" with a link back
  • Wallet, points, coupons, free delivery per zone are still visible
Full
  • COD, bKash, Nagad and card/mobile banking; bKash 5% / Nagad 3% method discounts; bKash cashback event banner
  • Advance payment on COD orders: a non-free delivery fee is paid in advance; plus an advance % for dropship / flagged items (20-30%), items above ৳50,000 (10%) and risky customers (25%); when several apply their %s are added together, capped at 100% (Q111). "Pay now" breakdown shown in the checkout summary; demo buttons at the bottom of checkout toggle each case
  • Pre-order products, badges and the 20% pre-order advance
  • Online payment page with the 30:00 countdown and mock gateway

Free delivery (Q105)

Free delivery means the customer pays no delivery fee. The threshold is per zone: Inside Dhaka free above ৳1,499, Dhaka suburbs above ৳2,999, outside Dhaka never. The cart progress bar uses the default address zone (Inside Dhaka for guests) and is hidden for zones that are never free.
