Owner Manual

How to run your online shop from the admin panel: take orders, send parcels, manage products, run offers, pay suppliers and watch your sales reps. No technical knowledge needed.

The admin panel is in EnglishScreen names and buttons are in English. This manual keeps them in English (in quotes) so you can match them on screen. Your customers see the shop in English or Bangla.

1. Getting started

Signing in

  1. Open the admin address your developer gave you and enter your email and password.
  2. Two-step verification (2FA): the first time, you scan a QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy). From then on you type the 6-digit code from the app after your password.
  3. Save your 10 backup codes shown at setup (print or write them down, keep them private). Each works once, if you lose your phone.
Lost phone and no backup codes?Another staff member with permission can reset your 2FA, or ask your developer. You can only be locked out, never see someone else’s data.
  • You are signed out automatically after 8 hours without activity (and always after 7 days).
  • If you sign in from a new computer or phone, you get an email alert. If it was not you, change your password immediately.
  • Too many wrong passwords temporarily blocks sign-in (about 15 minutes).
  • Top-right account menu: change password and sign out. The theme switch changes light / dark / automatic.

3. A typical day

  1. Morning: open Call queue. Phone each flagged customer and record the result (section 5).
  2. Open Orders → filter new ones. Check anything on hold or unusual.
  3. Open Shipments. Pack ready orders, book the courier, print labels and invoices, hand parcels to the courier (section 6).
  4. Inventory: look at low-stock items and plan purchases (section 9).
  5. Returns & refunds: approve, receive and inspect returns (section 7).
  6. Collections and SR approvals if you sell through sales reps (section 12).

4. Orders

Customers pay Cash on Delivery (COD) today. Online payment (bKash, Nagad, cards) is not switched on yet.

You do not set “shipped” or “delivered” by hand. They follow the shipments automatically.

Working an order

  • Orders lists all orders with filters and search. Click one to open the detail page: customer, delivery address, items, discounts, payment, shipments, and a history of every status change with who did it.
  • Hold / Resume: pause an order, with a reason.
  • Cancel: asks for a reason. Not possible while a courier booking exists. Cancel the shipment first.
  • Invoice: create and print an A4 invoice (VAT included in price; your BIN and VAT show if filled in Settings). The invoice is frozen when issued, so reprints always match.
  • Prices on an order never change later, even if you edit the product or coupon afterwards.

5. Call queue

Not every order needs a call. The system flags only risky ones:

  • the same phone or address ordering many times in a day;
  • a cash-on-delivery total above ৳5,000 (changeable);
  • a customer’s first guest (not logged-in) order.

The order still goes ahead. The queue only reminds you to confirm. For each one press “Log call” and choose the result:

ResultEffect
ConfirmedOrder continues normally.
UnreachableRecorded; try again later or cancel.
CancelledCustomer said no; the order is cancelled.

Add a note. It stays on the order. You can change the thresholds in Settings → call queue.

6. Shipments and couriers

  1. On Shipments (or the order page) create a shipment from the order’s items. You can split an order into several parcels.
  2. Book courier: pick the courier (Steadfast, Pathao). The system sends the parcel details and stores the consignment number and tracking link.
  3. Print the 4x6 inch label (order number, address, cash to collect, barcode) and the invoice; stick the label on the parcel.
  4. Mark handed over when the courier takes it. Stock leaves your warehouse count now.
  5. Sync status pulls the latest courier status. This also happens automatically every 10 minutes.
  • The delivery charge is collected once, on the first parcel sent.
  • If the courier cannot deliver (failed), the order is not cancelled automatically. Decide: re-book, or cancel.
  • The real courier connections need your merchant API keys from Steadfast / Pathao. Until the developer adds them, only a test “fake” courier works. Ask your developer to set these up before launch.

7. Returns and refunds

Requested→Approve / Reject→Pickup booked→Mark received→Inspect→Refund→Close return
  • A customer can request a return from their account within 7 days of delivery (change in Settings; a product or category can have its own).
  • Inspect: for each item choose resellable (goes back to stock with “Restock”) or damaged (not restocked).
  • Fault: “ours” (wrong/damaged item) means you pay return shipping. “customer” (changed mind) means the customer pays.
  • Refund amount is worked out per item after the discount that item received. Refund to “original” method or “wallet”. Because only COD is live, you pay the money back yourself, then press Mark paid out.
  • Approval rule: refunds above ৳3,000 (changeable) must be approved by a different staff member who has the “approve refunds” permission. This protects you from one person refunding to themselves.

8. Products and catalog

Adding a product

  1. Catalog → Products → New product. Tabs: Basics (English/Bangla title, brand, category, status), Description (rich text, EN/BN), Attributes (specs like “Material”), Variants, images (Media), Shipping (weight, delivery charge override, return window).
  2. Variants: a variant is one sellable version (e.g. “Red / M”). Add up to two options (Colour, Size) and the grid of combinations appears. A product with no options has one variant. Each variant has its own SKU (unique code), price, optional old price (shown struck through), sale price with start/end dates, cost price (only people with permission see it), and weight.
  3. Enter all money in taka (e.g. 125.50).
  4. Images: upload; drag to reorder; the first is the main image. Large or very high-resolution images are rejected or shrunk, and camera location data is removed.
  5. Status: draft (hidden), active (visible in shop), archived (hidden, history kept). Products that have been ordered cannot be deleted. Archive them.
  6. Save. If something is missing, a “Problems” list tells you what.

9. Promotions

Coupons & discounts

Two kinds: Code (customer types it, e.g. EID10) and Automatic (applies by itself when conditions match). Types:

TypeExample
Percent10% off, max ৳500 discount
Fixed৳100 off
TieredSpend ৳2,000 get ৳100; spend ৳5,000 get ৳400
Free deliveryNo delivery charge
Buy X get YBuy 2 get 1 half-price
BundleThese 3 items together for ৳999

Controls: start/end dates, minimum order, which products/categories/brands (include or exclude), which delivery zones, which customers (all, first order, specific, inactive for N days), web/app, total uses, uses per customer/phone/device, total budget, and whether it combines with sale prices or other coupons. Statuses: draft → active → paused. “Hidden” coupons are not listed publicly; “public” ones appear on the shop’s offers page and customers can collect them. The Report tab shows uses and discount given. You can generate many one-time codes at once.

Rule of thumb: a customer gets all automatic discounts that combine, plus at most one typed code (two only if both allow combining).

Flash sales

Pick a time window, add variants with a special price and a quantity limit. A countdown shows in the shop. When the limit is gone (or the customer’s own limit is reached) the shop will not sell more at that price. Orders keep the flash price they were placed at.

Bulk sales

Put a whole category, brand or list of products on sale in one go: percent or fixed amount off, with dates. Use Preview first. It shows the new prices. Undo restores previous sale prices exactly.

Home & feed

Manage home page carousel slides: desktop and mobile image, link (e.g. /p/product-name), start/end dates, language (all / English / Bangla), on/off, order. Home feed ordering weights are also managed under this menu (needs the “content” permission).

10. Suppliers and purchase orders

  1. Suppliers & POs: add suppliers (contact, payment terms, “ship mode”: normally Via our warehouse first). On a supplier page: details, the products they supply (with last price), and the ledger (what you owe).
  2. Purchase orders → New: choose supplier, add variants and quantities and unit cost. Save as draft, edit freely.
  3. Send to supplier: the PO is locked and emailed (if the supplier has an email). Use the A4 print view to hand or send it otherwise.
  4. Receive goods when stock arrives: enter good quantity and rejected quantity per line. You cannot receive more than ordered. Only good items go into stock and into what you owe. Your item cost price updates automatically as a weighted average.
  5. If the rest will never come, use Close PO: nothing more is owed for the missing part.
  6. Pay the supplier: on the supplier’s ledger record a payment (cash / bKash / Nagad / bank + reference). Use an adjustment for credit notes or opening balances. Entries cannot be edited. A mistake is corrected with a reversing entry.

Statuses: draft → sent → partially_received → received (or closed / cancelled). The PO list also shows “received value”, the money value of what truly arrived.

11. Sales reps (SR) and wholesale shops (B2B)

Your SRs use the separate partner portal on their phone to sell to local shops on credit. In the admin you control approval, delivery and money.

  1. SR submits a sale memo for a shop (shops added from the field are marked “new” for your review).
  2. SR approvals: approve (stock is reserved) or reject with a reason.
  3. Mark delivered (by SR or by staff). Now the shop owes the amount (minus any paid at sale).
  4. Collections: the SR records money collected from a shop. You confirm it when the cash really reaches you, or reject it with a reason. Only confirmed money reduces the shop’s due.
  5. B2B dues: list of who owes how much and how old, so you know whom to chase. B2B shops page shows each shop’s ledger and lets you block a shop.
  6. SR team: set area, monthly target and commission (default 1% of money collected, per-SR override). If a sale is reversed after commission was counted, a deduction is applied to the next commission.

12. Delivery charges

Delivery & couriers controls what customers pay for delivery. A zone has a base charge (for a base weight, default 1 kg), an extra charge per additional kg, estimated delivery days, and an optional “free when order reaches ৳X” (or always free). “Coverage” assigns districts, upazilas or areas to the zone. Every location belongs to one zone; one zone is the default and catches everything unassigned.

13. Staff, roles and settings

Staff and roles

  • Staff: add people, assign roles, disable (not delete) someone who leaves, reset their 2FA.
  • Roles & permissions: a role is a named set of permissions (e.g. “Warehouse”: inventory + shipments + receiving). A person can have several roles. The built-in Owner role has everything and cannot be edited or removed; the last Owner cannot be removed.
  • You can only give someone permissions that you have yourself.

Suggested starting roles: Manager (everything but staff/roles/settings), Order desk (orders, call queue, returns), Warehouse (shipments, inventory, receiving), Catalog editor (products, categories, brands), Accountant (view orders, supplier payments, B2B dues). Give cost prices and refund approval only to trusted people.

15. Good habits and safety

  • Give each person their own account. Never share a login.
  • Disable accounts the same day someone leaves.
  • Keep 2FA on for everyone. Keep backup codes safe.
  • Ask your developer to back up the database regularly and to keep the 2FA encryption key safe.
  • Use “Preview” and “Undo” on bulk sales; test a new coupon with a small budget cap first.
  • Count stock physically now and then and use “Adjust” with a note to fix differences.

16. Not available yet

  • Real SMS delivery of OTP codes. A provider must be chosen before launch.
  • Live Steadfast / Pathao booking (needs your merchant keys).
  • Sales reports and a working dashboard.
  • Supplier portal and direct-from-supplier (dropship) selling, affiliate program, product reviews, abandoned-cart reminders, events and page builder.
  • A staff-activity audit log (the order, stock and ledger histories do track who did what).
